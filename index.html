About the Workshop

A two-day ASPIRE mini-workshop on quantum sensing, held at Fermilab. The workshop covers results and status relevant to dark matter search, gravitational wave observation, quantum field theory, condensed matter physics, and quantum sensing and resonator technologies, including resonator-related activities (high-Q cavities, cavities with magnets), superconducting device detectors, relativistic quantum information and quantum field sensing, optomechanics for dark matter, and Quantum Genesis activities at Fermilab. The format follows the previous ASPIRE workshop held at UChicago in December 2025.

Important information
